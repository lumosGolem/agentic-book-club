## Chapter 5 - Final Chapter.

### Scene I — Mantua. A street

Romeo, now in exile in Mantua, has a dream foreshadowing his death yet revival through Juliet. His servant Balthasar arrives with news from Verona, reporting that Juliet is dead and placed in the Capulet tomb. Crucially, Romeo does not receive Friar Lawrence’s message explaining the plan.

Overcome with grief, Romeo immediately resolves to die beside Juliet. He purchases poison from an apothecary, ignoring legal restrictions, demonstrating both despair and impulsiveness. He sets out for Verona, fully committed to ending his life.

***

### Scene II — Friar Lawrence’s cell

Friar Lawrence learns that his letter explaining Juliet’s feigned death never reached Romeo because the messenger was quarantined and unable to deliver it. The failure of communication is decisive.

Recognising the danger, Friar Lawrence plans to go to the Capulet tomb to be present when Juliet awakens and to protect her until Romeo can be informed. This scene establishes that the tragedy results from disrupted plans rather than intent.

***

### Scene III — A churchyard; the Capulet monument

Paris visits Juliet’s tomb to mourn. Romeo arrives, intending to die beside Juliet. Paris confronts him, believing Romeo has come to dishonour the Capulets. They fight, and Romeo kills Paris, granting his request to be placed near Juliet.

Romeo enters the tomb, sees Juliet, and believes she is truly dead. He delivers a final speech, drinks the poison, and dies beside her.

Friar Lawrence arrives as Juliet awakens. He explains the situation and urges her to leave. Juliet refuses to go. After the Friar departs, she sees Romeo dead, takes his dagger, and kills herself.

The Prince, Capulets, and Montagues arrive. Friar Lawrence reveals the full sequence of events: the secret marriage, the plan, and the failed message. The deaths of Romeo and Juliet reconcile the two families. Montague and Capulet agree to end their feud and honour the lovers’ memory.

***

**Overall progression of Chapter V:**

* Resolves the plot through failed communication and irreversible decisions
* Transforms private tragedy into public revelation
* Completes the arc of fate established in the Prologue
* Ends the feud through the deaths of the lovers, fulfilling the “star-crossed” destiny
* Confirms that haste, miscommunication, and entrenched conflict produce catastrophic outcomes
